Sloth ToDo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Sloth ToDo is a to-do list app. The app itself collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. The only third party involved is Google, which serves the ads in the app.

Information the app stores

Your tasks (title, notes, due date, priority and whether each one is done) and your sort preference are stored only on your own device.

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
  • There is no server. We do not operate a backend, and your data is never uploaded to us.
  • We cannot see your tasks. Nobody can retrieve them from us, because we never receive them.
  • The app works fully offline.

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. It is not backed up to us and cannot be restored by us. If your device backs itself up (to iCloud on an iPhone, or to your Google account on Android), your Sloth ToDo data may be included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not this one.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, transmit or have access to:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • the content of your tasks
  • usage analytics of our own - the app contains no analytics or telemetry SDK (see Advertising below for what Google's ad SDK collects)

Advertising

Sloth ToDo is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes certain information. According to Google's own disclosure, this includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address, used to estimate coarse location and for diagnostics
  • advertising data, and data about how you interact with ads
  • crash logs and performance data from the ads SDK itself

This is collected by Google, not by us - we never see it and never receive it.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Tracking and your choice (iOS)

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before any tracking that uses your device's advertising identifier, using Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency prompt.

You can decline. If you do, you will still see ads and the app works exactly the same - the ads are simply less personalised. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Android

Sloth ToDo is also available on Google Play for Android phones. It stores the same data in the same way, only on your device.

  • Ads. Google's Mobile Ads SDK for Android collects, according to Google's Play data disclosure: approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions with ads, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers. Google uses this for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and security.
  • Advertising ID. The Android app can read your device's advertising ID so that Google can show ads chosen for you. Android does not show an in-app question for this. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (on most phones Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads); after you delete it, the ads are no longer personalised with it.
  • Permissions. The app uses only internet and network-state access, both needed to load the ads, and the advertising-ID permission. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or storage.
  • Backup. Android may include the app's data in your phone's backup to your Google account, so a new phone restored from that backup may bring your tasks back.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal information, because we do not collect any. The only data flow out of the app is the advertising request described above, which goes from your device to Google.

Children

Sloth ToDo is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

  • Access - everything is visible in the app.
  • Deletion - delete individual tasks and lists in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

There is no request to make to us, because there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.

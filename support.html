Need help, found a bug, or want to suggest something? Email binary.force.ca@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your phone's model, its iOS or Android version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a task?

Tap the + button on the main screen, type a title, and optionally add notes, a due date and a priority (Low, Medium or High). Tap a task to see its details, and edit it from there.

How do I find, filter and sort tasks?

Use the All, Today and Completed tabs to filter, the search button to search by text, and the sort button to order tasks by priority, due date, both, or alphabetically. The app remembers your sort choice.

Is my data backed up?

Not by us. Your tasks are stored only on your phone. There is no account and no server, which means we cannot see them, and equally cannot recover them for you. Deleting the app deletes them. If your iPhone backs itself up to iCloud, or your Android phone backs up to your Google account, the app's data may be included in that backup, and a new phone set up from it may bring it back.

Why is there an ad? Can I remove it?

Sloth ToDo is free and supported by one small banner at the bottom of the screen. There is no way to remove the banner in this version. The privacy policy explains what Google's ad SDK collects.

Why does the app ask to track me? (iPhone)

Apple's tracking permission lets Google show ads chosen for you. Saying "Ask App Not to Track" is fine: the app works the same, and the ads are chosen from the context instead. You can change your answer any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Can I stop personalised ads on Android?

Yes. Android does not ask inside the app; instead, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on most phones Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads). The app works the same either way.

I saw an ad that seems wrong. How do I report it?

Write to binary.force.ca@gmail.com with what the ad showed and when you saw it; a screenshot helps.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Only the banner needs a connection. Without one, the app works exactly the same and the space at the bottom stays empty.

Does it work on Android?

Yes, on Android phones, with the same features. Your tasks stay on the phone they were created on; they don't move between an iPhone and an Android phone. The system Back gesture or button takes you back a screen.
